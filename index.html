Security & trust

You're trusting HisabOne with tax documents and filings. Here's exactly how we protect them — described honestly, with no invented badges — together with our full Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and AI disclaimer.

Encrypted in transit & at rest

Every request is served over TLS, and your files and records are encrypted at rest in managed cloud storage.

Row-level security

Every record is scoped to your account in the database itself. Businesses, accountants and team members see only what their role permits — not just hidden in the UI.

Private document vault

Uploads live in a private bucket, never public. When you share a document we generate a time-limited signed link — so access is deliberate and revocable.

AI that respects your data

Document reading runs on Anthropic's Claude, used only to extract the dates you asked for from your file — never to train models.

Full audit trail

Every document access, every signature and every change is logged and timestamped, so there's always a clear record of who did what, and when.

Your data stays yours

Export your records anytime, and delete your account and its data whenever you choose.

Where your data lives

HisabOne runs on a small set of trusted infrastructure providers. We keep this list current so you always know who processes your data:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and encrypted file storage (hosted in the EU / Frankfurt region).
  • Vercel — application hosting and global content delivery.
  • Anthropic — the Claude AI model that reads dates from uploaded documents.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and reminder emails.

Honest about our stage

HisabOne is in private preview. We follow security best practices — encryption, least-privilege access and full audit logging — but we don't yet hold formal certifications such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. Those are on our roadmap, and we'd rather tell you plainly than display a badge we haven't earned.

AI can make mistakes — our disclaimer

AI can make mistakes. HisabOne includes artificial-intelligence features: automatic document and date reading, invoice OCR and expense auto-categorisation, the AI Assist chat, the penalty-exposure checker, and the VAT and Corporate Tax calculators and draft returns. You acknowledge and agree that:

  • AI-generated output may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, even when it appears confident and precise.
  • Document reading and OCR may misread amounts, dates, names, categories or TRNs — especially on low-quality scans.
  • The calculators and draft returns simplify rules that carry conditions, exceptions and transitional provisions; they are preparation aids, not filings.
  • HisabOne does not keep a ledger or maintain your books. Draft returns are built from the period totals you enter yourself and from the documents you upload, and are only ever as accurate as those figures.
  • AI Assist answers are general guidance only and are not tax, legal, accounting or audit advice.
  • You must verify every AI-generated figure against your source documents, and every deadline, eligibility conclusion and computation with the FTA's EmaraTax portal or a qualified professional adviser, before relying on it or filing.
  • AI output is not a substitute for professional review, and to the maximum extent permitted by UAE law we are not liable for losses arising from reliance on unverified AI output.

If you spot a suspected AI error, please report it to info@hisabone.ae so we can investigate.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Who we are

HisabOne (“we”, “us”) provides a UAE tax & compliance workspace connecting businesses and their accountants, available at hisabone.ae. For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@hisabone.ae.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name or company name, email address and password (stored only as a secure cryptographic hash by our authentication provider). While you use HisabOne we store the compliance data you choose to put in it: company profile details, document requests, deadlines, reminders, reports, draft returns, messages, and the company documents you upload to your vault.

Lawful basis — your consent

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (the PDPL), consent is the primary lawful basis for processing personal data. By creating a HisabOne account and using its features you consent to the processing described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by closing your account, after which your data is deleted as described below.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the service — showing your own workspace, sharing records you explicitly share with your linked accountant or clients, and sending the emails you ask us to send (such as document requests and reminders). We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

AI processing

Some features send data to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to work: automatic document and date reading, invoice OCR and expense categorisation, and the AI Assist chat (which receives a short summary of your workspace so its answers are specific to you). This data is processed only to provide the feature and is not used to train AI models. AI output can contain mistakes — see the AI accuracy disclaimer in our Terms of Use.

Where your data lives — international transfers

Your account and workspace data is stored with Supabase (managed PostgreSQL and encrypted file storage) in the EU — Frankfurt region. The website is served by Vercel's global network; AI requests are processed by Anthropic (United States); and email is delivered by Resend (United States). Because these providers operate outside the UAE, using HisabOne involves cross-border transfers of your data as contemplated by Articles 22–23 of the PDPL. We protect those transfers with TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest, contractual data-processing terms with each provider, and by sending each provider only the minimum data its function needs. You consent to these transfers by using the service.

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your profile, workspace records and uploaded files from our systems; residual copies in encrypted backups expire on the backup provider's rotation schedule.

Your rights

In line with the PDPL, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time, and you may object to or ask about any automated processing we perform, by emailing info@hisabone.ae. We respond to every request.

Cookies & local storage

We use only the browser storage necessary to keep you signed in, plus privacy-friendly, cookie-less visit analytics (Vercel Web Analytics). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note the new date at the top of this page. Questions? Email us.
